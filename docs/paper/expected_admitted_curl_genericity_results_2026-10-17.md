# Exact rational audit of the admitted-field curl zero set

**Status:** completed exact-arithmetic audit of a theorem corollary in the
existing canonical per-expert clipping model. This is not a new controller,
admission rule, or paper direction.

## Question

For the `E=3, K=1, T=6, C=(2,2,2)` iid-Gumbel model in
[`topk_expected_admission_jacobian_theorem_2026-10-17.md`](topk_expected_admission_jacobian_theorem_2026-10-17.md),
does the exact quotient curl vanish anywhere in the interior beyond the
pairwise-symmetry sets identified by the factorization?

## Exact calculation and controls

The runner evaluates every quantity with Python `Fraction`; it does not use
finite differences, Monte Carlo, or floating-point thresholds. For each
positive integer composition `(n1,n2,n3)` of a grid denominator `N`, it sets
`p=(n1,n2,n3)/N`, computes the exact binomial attenuation
`alpha(p)=Pr[Binomial(5,p)<2]`, and evaluates the exact quotient curl.
The runner asserts both (i) equality with the symbolic factorization and
(ii) `curl=0` iff at least two coordinates of `p` are equal.

Denominators used were `30, 60, 120, 240`. The exact controls were:

- the theorem witness `p=(1/2,1/3,1/6)`, which must give `-29/2187`;
- the symmetric point `p=(1/3,1/3,1/3)`, which must give zero;
- the same witness with no clipping (`C_e=T`), which must give zero.

## Results

All exact assertions passed.

| Grid denominator | Interior points | Zero curl | Nonzero curl | Positive / negative |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 30 | 406 | 40 | 366 | 183 / 183 |
| 60 | 1,711 | 85 | 1,626 | 813 / 813 |
| 120 | 7,021 | 175 | 6,846 | 3,423 / 3,423 |
| 240 | 28,441 | 355 | 28,086 | 14,043 / 14,043 |

Across all 37,579 rational points, the exact zero set on the grids consisted
only of points with a repeated probability coordinate. The witness and both
controls returned their required exact values. The output is
[`results/expected_admitted_generic_curl/audit.json`](../../results/expected_admitted_generic_curl/audit.json),
generated by
[`scripts/audit_expected_admitted_generic_curl.py`](../../scripts/audit_expected_admitted_generic_curl.py).

## Interpretation

The grid is a finite audit of the derived factorization; it does not prove
the continuum zero-set statement. That proof follows from
`Q=4s_2^2-2s_2-7s_3-1 <= -1` on the simplex, so the curl is zero exactly
when a pair of request probabilities is equal. The result rules out a
potential on every open pressure region for this specific stochastic
instance. It does not show cycles, instability, worse overflow, a training
quality effect, a telemetry price, or a separation from the independent
outcome-map benchmark.

The scope boundary is consistent with the primary literature: `$φ$-Balancing`
optimizes a potential of the expected routing distribution, while the
Gumbel-Top-k literature supplies the stochastic without-replacement routing
construction. Neither result, from the source material reviewed here,
establishes this clipped admitted-count curl statement; this targeted check
is not a systematic novelty review. See the official [PMLR paper page for
`$φ$-Balancing`](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/chen26br.html) and
[Kool et al., Gumbel-Top-k](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/kool19a.html).
